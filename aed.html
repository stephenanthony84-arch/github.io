AED Locations

The group manages and maintains a network of public access AEDs around the town and the broader Dingle peninsula.

Find an AED Near You

Knowing where your nearest Automated External Defibrillator is located can save a life in an emergency. Explore the interactive map to locate the nearest unit to your home, workplace, or route.

Please contact us if you notice damaged or missing AEDs, or if the locations of the AEDs noted on the map are incorrect.
