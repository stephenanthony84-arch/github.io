Who we are

Your neighbours, trained and ready

We are local business owners, teachers, farmers and retirees who live and work in and around Dingle. Every responder is trained and certified in CPR and the use of an AED.

Ambulances often travel a long way to reach us, so we bridge the gap in those first critical minutes as the first link in the chain of survival.

The AED network

Public defibrillators from Dún Chaoin to Lispole

We manage and maintain a network of public-access AEDs in and around Dingle: on pub walls, shop fronts, halls and schools, so a defibrillator is never far away.

  • 27public-access AEDs mapped in and around Dingle
  • 4kinds of emergency we are dispatched to: cardiac arrest, chest pain, stroke and choking
  • 112call first: the dispatcher tells you the nearest cabinet and its code if it is locked

When we are called

Four emergencies where minutes matter most

When a 999 or 112 call is made in or around Dingle for one of these, ambulance control dispatches our on-call volunteers at the same moment as the ambulance.

Cardiac arrest

Immediate CPR and an AED to restart the heart.

Chest pain

Monitoring, reassurance and readiness for sudden deterioration.

Stroke

Spotting the signs early with a FAST check so hospital transport is quick.

Choking

Clearing the airway and giving basic life support.

Community training

CPR and defibrillator workshops

So many people from the community turned up to our latest CPR and defibrillator workshop. As well as being vital training, the proceeds help us maintain the defibrillators in the area.

Participants practise chest compressions on manikins and see how a public-access AED guides you through each step. Students at Sacred Heart University in Dingle gave up their time to run the practical training.
