Be a Helping Hand in Your Community

At Dingle Heart Safe, our volunteers are simply neighbours helping neighbours. When someone on the peninsula experiences a medical emergency—such as a cardiac arrest, stroke, or a choking incident—we are dispatched at the same time as the ambulance.

Because we live locally, our goal is to arrive quickly to assist the patient and provide comfort in the moments before the ambulance crew arrives. We help by providing basic, practical support, like performing CPR or deploying an AED, to bridge the gap during the wait.

If you would like to support your community in a meaningful way, we would love to welcome you to our team. We provide full training, and absolutely no prior medical experience is needed.

Email us at info@dingleheartsafe.com to learn more about the process of joining.
