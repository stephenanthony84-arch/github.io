Join Our Team

Make a Direct Difference

Our volunteers offer comfort in the moments before the ambulance crew arrives. We help by providing basic, practical support, like performing CPR or deploying an AED, to bridge the gap during the wait.

If you would like to support your community in a meaningful way, we would love to welcome you to our team. We provide full, accredited training, and absolutely no prior medical experience is needed.

Email us at cfrdingle@gmail.com to learn more about the process of joining.
