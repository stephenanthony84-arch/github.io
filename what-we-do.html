What We Do

Core Operations

The team is dispatched simultaneously with the NAS via 999/112 calls, but specifically for four highly time-critical emergencies: Cardiac Arrest, Choking, Stroke, and Heart Attack.

Because our volunteers live and work within the Dingle community, we can often arrive on the scene within minutes, bridging the critical gap before professional medical help arrives.

Defibrillator Maintenance

The group plays a vital role in ensuring that public access AEDs across the Dingle Peninsula remain rescue-ready at all times. Volunteers regularly inspect and maintain defibrillators, replacing pads and other essential equipment as required.

By carrying out routine checks and coordinating repairs when needed, we help ensure that life-saving equipment is available and fully operational for anyone experiencing a cardiac emergency in our community.
