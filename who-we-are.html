Who We Are

A Community-Led Response

The Dingle Heart Safe Community First Responder (CFR) group is a volunteer organization directly linked to the National Ambulance Service. When emergency calls are made locally, our nearby responders are deployed simultaneously to provide immediate support before advanced crews arrive.

Composed completely of passionate local citizens, we dedicate our time to monitoring equipment, practicing emergency skills, and safeguarding lives throughout our peninsula.

Always Ready

Being a first responder isn't just about answering the call; it is about continuous preparation. Our team undergoes rigorous and regular training under the governance of the National Ambulance Service.

We train not only our own responders but the wider public, ensuring the Dingle Peninsula is a safer place for everyone who lives, works, and visits here.
